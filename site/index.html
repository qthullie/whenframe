    The model

    Déclic

    Déclic is the model whenframe searches with. Two image encoders that see differently, Google's SigLIP 2 and Meta's Perception Encoder, embed one frame per second on your computer's processor. Two small models that whenframe trained on more than 100,000 descriptions written by people rewrite each frame with what the frames around it show, so that an action is found, not only a picture. Frames that match almost any text are marked down, and the two encoders' scores are added. It found the shots of the film above.

    Where Déclic is not first: placing a moment in a video it was never trained on, a 7-billion-parameter vision-language model on a GPU does better. The protocol of each test was written before it ran, and the criteria it missed are listed with the ones it met: results · protocol.

    Install

    pip install git+https://github.com/qthullie/whenframe
whenframe index ~/Videos
whenframe search "the dog jumps into the pool"
whenframe film story.txt -o film.mp4
whenframe supercut "fireworks" -o fireworks.mp4

    Python 3.10+ and ffmpeg. The first run downloads the models (1.4 GB). film cuts one shot per line of a story, supercut every moment of one description in the order they were filmed; --music puts your own music under them.

    On your computer

    The videos are read, embedded and searched on your computer, and nothing is sent anywhere. Descriptions written in French are translated there too, then searched. whenframe serve opens the same search in your browser, and links each moment to Immich or Jellyfin.
